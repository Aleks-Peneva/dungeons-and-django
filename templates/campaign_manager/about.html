The West Marches Charter

Standing orders and ledger regulations for frontier expeditions

1. Rotating Expeditions

Unlike traditional campaigns with a fixed weekly table, the West Marches features a shared persistent world and a large pool of mercenaries. Players assemble their own parties based on open rumours on the Quest Board and schedule expeditions into uncharted hexes.

2. Mortality & The Ledger

The frontier is unforgiving. When an adventurer falls in battle, their status is marked as Fallen in the Guild Roster. Fallen heroes remain memorialised in the ledger, though guild rules forbid assigning deceased characters to new active quests.

3. Relic Attunement Rules

In accordance with D&D 5th Edition arcane laws, an adventurer may be attuned to no more than three (3) magic items simultaneously. The Guild Vault automatically enforces this limit when logging equipment.

4. True Names

Once an adventurer signs the Guild Charter, their Adventurer Name is permanently sealed in ink and cannot be altered when updating levels, status, or chronicle notes.
