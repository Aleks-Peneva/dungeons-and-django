About West Marches D&D

What to keep in mind when leading a West Marches campaign

1. Rotating Expeditions

Unlike traditional campaigns with a fixed player character party, the West Marches features a shared persistent world and a larger, rotating pool of players. Players assemble their own parties based on available Quests they're interested in.

2. Player Character Mortality

The world of a West Marches campaign is inherently not perfectly balanced, since more frequent players advance quicker. If a quests proves deadly and a player character falls in battle, their status is marked as Dead. Fallen heroes' memory is preserved, but they can sadly no longer be assigned to new active quests.

3. Item Attunement Rules

In accordance with D&D 5th Edition rules, a player character may be attuned to no more than three (3) magic items simultaneously. The campaign manager automatically enforces this limit when logging equipment.

4. Character Names

In order to avoid confusion at the different forming tables of player (and spare the dungeon master the inevitable constantly repeating annoying jokes), each Player Character Name must be unique and may not be altered.
